Privacy and disclaimer

Daily Log is a shift aid made by Erica Lucia Sanchez. Contact: sanchezsilas17@gmail.com.

Patient names, vitals, medications, intake, output, and notes are stored only in the browser on that phone. The app does not upload charts to Daily Log servers. A photo of a monitor is read on the phone to fill numbers. Do not use a shared or borrowed phone for real patients.

Export a backup before you clear Safari data. Erase all data from More inside the app. Uninstalling the Home Screen icon does not always delete the saved log. Use Erase if you leave a job or a phone.

This app is not an electronic health record, medication administration record, or medical device. It does not diagnose. Flags are reminders to report to the nurse. Follow facility policy. Copy required notes into the official chart. A CNA should log medications only when that task is within their role.

The pilot unlock code is a license for one phone. It is not insurance and not a facility contract. A building-wide account will be a separate agreement.
